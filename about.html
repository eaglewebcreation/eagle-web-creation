About Eagle Web Creation

A small, local web design business in northern Wyoming, built on a simple idea: a business website should be clean, quick, and easy for customers to use.

Who I am

I'm Talon Grant, and I build websites from northern Wyoming, often in the quiet hours after my newborn son finally falls asleep and before my wife, Saidey, tells me to close the laptop. When I'm not designing, you'll find me in the gym, battling it out on the pickleball court, chasing a basketball dunk I swear is coming any day now, or yelling at the TV during a big UFC card. Faith keeps me grounded, and Sunday mornings at church with my family are the part of the week I'd never trade. I bring that same mix of competitiveness and care to every site I build, because a small business deserves a website that works as hard as the people behind it.

When you work with me, you work with me directly. No sales team, no call center, and no handing your project off to someone you've never met.

How I build websites

I build every site from scratch instead of using drag-and-drop page builders. Here's why that matters for you.

  • It's fasterNo extra builder code loading in the background, so pages open quickly, even on a weak cell signal.
  • It's cleanerEvery page is laid out on purpose. Nothing on the screen that doesn't need to be there.
  • It's built for searchTitles, descriptions and page structure are written around the services you offer and the area you serve.
  • It's readable by AIYour services, prices and service area are written out and marked up so AI assistants can describe your business accurately.
  • It's set up properlyYour domain and DNS are configured correctly, so your site and web address work the way they should.
