What they needed

208 Trailer Rentals & Moving rents large 35-foot enclosed cargo trailers by the day or the week from a secured lot that's open around the clock.

The site needed to let customers see which trailer is free on their dates and reserve it themselves, explain how pickup works, and show movers, stagers and families why a trailer this size is worth it.

What I built

Availability by date

Customers pick a pickup and return date and see which trailers are free. Each trailer has its own calendar, so what they see is real availability.

Reserve and pay online

Customers choose a trailer and pay by card on the site, or call or text if they'd rather talk to someone.

Gate-code pickup at any hour

The site explains how the gate code is sent by text once a reservation is confirmed, so customers can pick up and return at any hour.

Every trailer, side by side

Each trailer is shown with its photos, size, features and daily and weekly rates, so customers can compare before they book.

Pages for movers and real estate

Moving companies, home stagers and real estate agents get a page of their own showing how the trailers fit their work.

Rental terms and answers up front

Rates, requirements, cancellation and the rental terms are spelled out, with a page of common questions.

Set up to be found

The site runs on the business's own domain, 208trailerrentalsandmovingllc.com. Trailers and booking, movers and real estate, rental terms and common questions each have a page of their own, so a search for a specific need can land on the page that answers it.
