What they needed

Blaze Home Loans is an independent mortgage broker licensed in Wyoming, Idaho, Montana, Colorado and Florida, and it already had a website.

The new one needed to represent the company as professionally as it operates, explain a wide range of loan options in plain words, show the people behind the company, and give real estate agents a reason to send buyers over.

What I built

A page for each loan option

Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA and DSCR loans each get a plain-language page, with more pages for HELOCs, bank-statement loans, reverse mortgages and fix-and-flip loans.

Location pages

Each market Blaze serves has its own page, so borrowers there can see that Blaze works in their area.

The team, up front

Every loan officer has a profile with their NMLS number, the states they cover and a direct way to call or email them.

A section for Realtors

Real estate agents get their own page and a simple way to introduce a buyer to Blaze.

Mortgage guides

Guides answer common questions, such as how a mortgage broker differs from a bank and how DSCR loans work for investors.

Licensing and disclosures

NMLS numbers, state licensing and the disclosures a mortgage company has to show are on the site, with a page of their own.

One clear next step

Talk to a loan officer opens Blaze's booking calendar, and the phone number and email are on every page.

New logos and marketing material

Beyond the website, I designed new logos for Blaze and created marketing material for the company.

Set up to be found

Each loan type, location and team member has a page of its own, so a search for a specific loan or a specific loan officer can land on the page that answers it.
