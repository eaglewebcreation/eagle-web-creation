KB Hardware & Lumber

A website for a hometown hardware store and lumber yard, built to answer the questions people would otherwise call about.

The website

A hometown hardware store's site with store hours that show if they're open right now, a quote request form for material lists, and a page for every department.

  • Live open/closed hours
  • Quote requests
  • Department pages

What they needed

KB Hardware & Lumber is a hardware store and lumber yard with six departments under one roof, from lumber and fasteners to paint and a licensed firearms counter.

The site needed to answer what people want to know before they drive in: whether the store is open, whether it carries what they need, and what a material list will cost.

What I built

Store hours that know the time

The top of every page shows whether the store is open right now and when it closes, worked out from the store's real hours.

A page for every department

Lumber, hardware, tools, plumbing and electrical, and paint each have their own page, so someone searching for one of them lands on the right page.

Quote requests

Customers send a material list for a build, and the store prices it out and calls them back before they come in.

A firearms page that changes every day

Every firearm in the case is listed with photos and prices, the homepage shows a different three each day, and customers can join an email list to hear when something new comes in.

Real Google reviews

Five-star Google reviews from customers rotate on the homepage, word for word, with a link to read them all on Google.

Their own logo, rebuilt

The store's existing logo was recreated so it stays sharp at any size, and it shows as the preview when someone shares the site.

Set up to be found

The site runs on the store's own domain, kbhardwarelumber.com. Each department page has its own search title and description, written around what that department sells.

A questions and answers page and a visit page with hours and directions cover the things people ask most.
