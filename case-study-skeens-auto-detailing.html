Case study

Skeens' Auto Detailing

A website for a mobile detailing business, built to show prices up front and make booking take a couple of minutes.

skeensautodetail.com

Mobile auto detailing · Cody, WY

The website

A mobile detailer's site with packages priced by vehicle size, online booking, photos of real work and Google reviews, on the business's own domain.

  • Prices by vehicle size
  • Online booking
  • Google reviews
Visit skeensautodetail.com (opens in a new tab)

What they needed

Skeens' Auto Detailing is a mobile detailing business that brings the shop to the customer's driveway, from interior details to paint correction and ceramic coatings.

The site needed to show prices up front, make booking quick, and let the work speak for itself with real photos and reviews.

What I built

Five packages, priced up front

Each detail level builds on the one before it, and the pricing page lets customers pick their vehicle size to see their price.

Booking in a couple of minutes

Every Book button goes straight to the business's online booking, and the phone number is one tap to call or text.

Photos of real work

A gallery shows recent details, all the business's own work, with no stock photos.

Reviews that do the selling

The Google rating and Facebook recommendations sit next to five-star Google reviews, quoted word for word.

Changes checked before they go live

Updates are made on a test copy of the site first and go live only once they've been checked.

Set up to be found

The site moved from a preview address to the business's own domain, skeensautodetail.com, and the old address redirects there so no link breaks.

It's verified in Google Search Console, has a sitemap, and carries structured data listing each service with its starting price. An llms.txt file gives AI search tools the services, prices and booking link in plain text.
