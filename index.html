How I build your site

A simple process, so you always know where things stand.

  1. We talk

    I learn what you do, where your customers are, and what you want the site to accomplish. That tells us which size of site fits.

  2. I plan the pages

    I map out each page and what goes on it, written around the services you offer and the words your customers actually search for.

  3. I build it

    Your site is custom-built, mobile-first, and designed around your logo and brand colors. No bloated page builders slowing it down.

  4. I set it up to be found

    More people now find local businesses by asking AI tools like ChatGPT, not just by searching Google. I structure your site so both can clearly understand what you do and where you work, which helps you show up when customers go looking.

  5. You review it

    You get a private preview link to look over on your phone and computer. We make changes until it's right.

  6. It goes live

    I connect your domain, set up the DNS correctly, and launch the site so it shows up where it should.

  7. I keep it current

    If you choose a management plan, you send changes when you need them and I take care of the rest.
