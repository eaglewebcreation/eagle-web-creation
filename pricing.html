Pricing questions

Do I pay for the whole website up front?

No. You see your whole site on a private preview first, and I don’t ask for the balance until you’ve reviewed it and you’re happy with it.

Is a monthly plan required?

Yes. Every site I build comes with a monthly plan. Starter is $75 a month and covers hosting, an SSL certificate, regular backups, security updates, uptime checks and 30 minutes of edits a month.

What if I need more changes than my plan covers?

Extra work is $100 per hour, billed in full hours. For example, an extra 40 minutes is billed as 1 hour.

How do I know which size of website I need?

Think about where your customers come from. If they’re mostly within about 60 miles, a 1-5 page site for $800 usually fits. If you advertise across the state, a 6-10 page site for $1,500 fits. If you sell in multiple states or online, the site is priced after a consultation.

Can I add features after my site is built?

Yes. Add-ons like a scroll animation, 3D models, a second language or a seasonal refresh can be added while your site is being built or later on.
