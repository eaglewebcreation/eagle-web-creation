Add it to Gmail

  1. Press Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear, then See all settings. On the General tab, scroll to Signature, click Create new, name it Eagle Web, and paste.
  3. Under Signature defaults, pick talon@eaglewebcreation.com, choose Eagle Web for new emails and for replies and forwards, then click Save Changes at the bottom.

If the copy button doesn't work in your browser, drag to select the whole signature above, copy it, and paste it into Gmail the same way.
