Competing in Billings search results

Billings is Montana’s largest city and the Yellowstone County seat, which means more customers and a lot more competition for every search. A slow or dated site loses people to the next result in seconds, so speed, clear pages and a strong first screen matter here more than anywhere.

I build each site around the services you want to be found for, with a page for each main service when it makes sense, clean structure, and your business details marked up for search engines and AI assistants. The Local SEO plan adds ongoing ranking work and a monthly report.
