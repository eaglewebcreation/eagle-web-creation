Livingston and Park County, Montana

Livingston is the Park County seat, about 156 miles from Lovell, roughly three hours by car. We can meet in person if it helps, and the rest happens by phone and email.

It started as a railroad town and now depends a lot on tourism, with Paradise Valley running about 50 miles south to the North Entrance at Gardiner. The Livingston Roundup Rodeo brings crowds every Fourth of July. Visitors are searching for a place to eat, stay or fish, and a clear site gets you on their list.
