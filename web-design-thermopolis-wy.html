Websites for a visitor town

Thermopolis is the Hot Springs County seat, about 100 miles south of Lovell through Greybull, Basin and Worland, roughly a two-hour drive.

Tourism is a big part of the economy here. Hot Springs State Park and the Big Spring, which the town calls the world’s largest mineral hot spring, draw visitors, along with the Wyoming Dinosaur Center. The Gift of the Waters Pageant is held at the Big Spring the first weekend in August.
